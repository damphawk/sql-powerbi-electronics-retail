# sql-powerbi-electronics-retail
SQL and Power BI analysis of electronics retail

Where does this retailer make money, who are the best customers, and where is it losing value?

Business Questions:
1) What percent of customers are repeat customers? What is the average time between buying for these customers?
2) Which 5 products earn the most profit within each category?
3) What is year-over-year revenue growth by country, online versus retail stores?
4) What are the unsold products, inactive stores, and customers without purchases?
5) What is the average order value and product category of different age groups?
